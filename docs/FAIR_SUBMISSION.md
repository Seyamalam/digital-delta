# Bangladesh Innovation Fair 2026 submission brief

Checked on 4 September 2026 against the official fair website.

## Confirmed public requirements

- The fair lists Novo Theatre, Dhaka, as its venue.
- The innovator application is currently closed.
- The application requires a PowerPoint file with 7 to 8 slides.
- The requested judging topics are originality, practical impact, commercialization potential, technical capability, contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals, innovative leadership, social inclusion, ethics and safety, and market demand.
- The form accepts PPT and PPTX files and permits one optional supporting document and one optional project photo.
- Applicants retain the intellectual property they lawfully own. Submitted non-confidential information may be used for evaluation, programme preparation, approved public display, media, and fair publications.

Sources:

## Items that still require organizer confirmation

- selection or participation status for this submission;
- final event date and reporting time;
- live pitch duration and question period;
- equipment, table, power socket, internet, sound, and projector rules;
- whether an independent innovator receives a kiosk or must book an exhibitor package;
- submission reopening or late-submission procedure.

The public site labels its schedule as proposed. Some event-detail pages expose old or placeholder dates and contact information, so those pages are not treated as current confirmation.

## Project fit

The strongest application categories are Climate Environment and Sustainability and Information and Digital Technology. Health Medical and Life Sciences is a supporting category because the demo centers on urgent medical supplies, but the system does not perform clinical triage.

The eight-slide deck should map directly to the application criteria:

1. Flood logistics problem and source-backed evidence
2. Working bilingual field system
3. Offline architecture and technical implementation
4. Live failure demonstration across all eight modules
5. Safety privacy and accessibility
6. Users market demand and commercialization
7. Pilot plan social inclusion and SDG contribution
8. Evidence limitations and requested partnership
