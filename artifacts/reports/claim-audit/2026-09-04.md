# Public claim audit

Date: 4 September 2026

Scope: README, final report, pitch deck, screenshots, demo manuscript, and public headquarters copy

## Claims retained

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary shown |
|---|---|---|
| All eight module paths are implemented | Android, Go, dashboard, archive, and model tests; traceability matrix | Physical field verification remains open |
| Bangla and English ship together | Paired Android resources, local parity gate, connected journeys, paired captures | Human task-completion review remains open |
| Field work does not depend on the command laptop | Android-owned Room, Keystore, routing, triage, and custody paths | Physical laptop-disconnection proof remains open |
| Mesh payloads are encrypted Protobuf | Shared schema, mesh source gate, recipient-only decryption test | Radio range is not claimed |
| The route-risk model runs on device | Reproducible ONNX artifact and connected inference journey | Training data is visibly synthetic |
| Ten thousand gRPC streams were acknowledged | Recorded load report and reproducible harness | p95 was 57.645 seconds; no district-throughput claim |
| Sixty connected journeys pass on Android 15 and 16 | Final unchanged local gate report | Emulator evidence, not target-phone evidence |

## Claims removed or narrowed

- The unsupported claim that 5.2 million people were displaced in 72 hours in July 2025 is not used in the public pitch.
- Simulated rainfall, saturation, edge failure, boat movement, and drone movement are not presented as live observations.
- The system is not called field ready, production ready, a medical-triage replacement, or a drone controller.
- The project does not claim to be the first or only disaster logistics platform.
- The 10,000-stream result is described as laptop connection-capacity evidence, not operational radio throughput.

## Release-gate wording

Every final artifact keeps physical radio, camera, target-phone performance, accessibility review, organizer confirmation, and rehearsal evidence open. No open gate is counted as completed software evidence.
