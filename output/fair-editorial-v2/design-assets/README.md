# Digital Delta design assets

Premium editorial identity for the Innovation Fair theme **Innovate to Market**. Prepared 4 September 2026.

## Edit and reuse

- Open `Digital-Delta-Diagrams.drawio` in draw.io/diagrams.net. Its twelve pages contain editable text, boxes and connectors.
- Use the numbered SVG files for crisp placement. Corresponding PNG exports are in `png/`.
- The PowerPoint contains editable text, diagrams, three native tables and two native charts with embedded data workbooks. Slides 1–14 form the main pitch; slides 15–30 support questions. Sources and speaking guidance are in the slide notes.
- The Word report has editable text and tables. Replace its diagram images after editing the corresponding draw.io or SVG source.

## Identity

The river-branch mark joins a river delta with connected endpoints. Use `delta-mark-green.svg` on light backgrounds, `delta-mark-white.svg` on dark backgrounds and the black version for monochrome printing. `digital-delta-lockup.svg` includes the wordmark. Preserve proportions and leave at least one endpoint diameter of clear space on each side. Keep the mark at least 24 pixels high on screen or 8 mm high in print.

Georgia provides the editorial headlines; Arial handles body copy, diagrams and data. The report uses white pages for print. Fonts are not redistributed. Install these fonts on the editing computer to preserve the layout. Bangla in the report and presentation is preserved in the original app screenshots.

The five original outline icons in `icons/` share a 24-unit grid, a 1.75-unit stroke and round caps. Use one colour per icon and retain a text label where meaning matters.

## Images and evidence

`illustrations/` contains three AI-generated concept images made with the built-in image-generation tool. They depict an imagined setting, not project deployment. Two new prompts are recorded in `image-prompts.json`; the river image is reused from the first edition. Keep concept-art captions when reusing them.

`screenshots/` contains 26 unaltered original captures used by the report. `screenshot-sources.json` maps them to repository files and report pages. Some filenames describe a target resolution even when the stored image is smaller; the files are preserved as supplied. `details/` contains enlarged-detail crops. Report crop coordinates are recorded in `crop-sources.json`. The three `pod-detail-*` images crop the matching accepted, replay-rejected and tamper-rejected source captures on report page 22, using left 2.5%, top 47%, width 95% and height 36% of each source.

The application captures contain simulated environmental, cargo and vehicle states. Preserve those labels and the OpenStreetMap attribution. The physical multi-phone relay and camera acceptance gates remain separate from emulator evidence.

The diagrams summarize documented software responsibilities. Buyer roles, service packaging, the twelve-week pilot and budget are proposals. The model chart uses held-out synthetic data. The report carries the complete research and repository source registers on pages 39–40.
